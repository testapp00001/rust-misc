## Important Note

Solutions are provided for learning and self-assessment. The recommended approach is:

1. Complete each exercise on your own first.
2. Compare your answer to the solution.
3. Understand the differences and reasoning behind the solution approach.
4. Experiment with variations and improvements beyond the provided solutions.
