## How to Use These Solutions

1. **Attempt each exercise before reading the solution.** The learning
   happens in the struggle, not in the reading.
2. **Study the reasoning, not just the code.** Every solution explains
   *why* a decision was made, not just *what* was implemented.
3. **Read the Common Mistakes section.** These are drawn from real
   production incidents and interview failures.
4. **Build on the solutions.** Use them as a starting point, then extend
   them with your own improvements and variations.
