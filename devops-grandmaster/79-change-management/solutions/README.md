## How to Use These Solutions

1. **Attempt each exercise first.** The exercises are designed for learning;
   reading solutions without trying defeats the purpose.
2. **Compare your work** against the solution to identify gaps and alternative
   approaches.
3. **Read the rationale sections.** Every solution explains _why_ certain
   decisions were made, not just _what_ was implemented.
4. **Adapt, don't copy.** Production environments have unique constraints --
   use these solutions as a starting point, not a drop-in replacement.

## Prerequisites

Same as the exercises directory. Ensure your Kubernetes cluster is available
before attempting solutions 2-5.
