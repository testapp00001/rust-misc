## How to Use These Solutions

1. **Attempt the exercise first.** The exercises are designed to build understanding
   through struggle. Looking at the solution too early defeats the purpose.
2. **Compare approaches.** If your answer differs from the solution, that is fine.
   Understand *why* both approaches work.
3. **Read the "Common Mistakes" sections.** These are drawn from real production
   incidents and are as valuable as the correct answer.
4. **Review the "Why This Works" sections.** Understanding the reasoning is more
   important than memorizing the answer.
