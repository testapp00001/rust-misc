## How to Use These Solutions

1. **Attempt the exercise first.** The solutions are not a substitute for thinking through the problem yourself.
2. **Compare approaches.** Your solution may differ from the reference -- both can be correct.
3. **Read the "Common Mistakes" sections.** These reflect real errors that teams make in production pipelines.
4. **Review the "Why This Works" sections.** Understanding the reasoning is more valuable than copying the YAML.
