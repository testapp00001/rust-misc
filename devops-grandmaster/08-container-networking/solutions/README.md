## How to Use These Solutions

1. **Attempt the exercise first.** These solutions are most useful after you
   have struggled with the problem yourself.
2. **Compare your answer.** If your answer differs, that is fine -- understand
   *why* both approaches work.
3. **Read the "Why It Works" section.** The goal is understanding, not copying.
4. **Check the "Common Mistakes" section.** These are the pitfalls you will
   hit in real-world Docker networking.
