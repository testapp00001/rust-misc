## How to Use These Solutions

1. Attempt the exercise before reading the solution.
2. Compare your answer to the solution.
3. If your answer differs, understand *why* both approaches work.
4. Pay attention to the "Common Mistakes" sections -- they highlight misconceptions that the exercise is designed to correct.
