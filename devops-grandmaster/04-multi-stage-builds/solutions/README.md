## How to Use These Solutions

1. Attempt each exercise before looking at the solution.
2. Compare your answer to the solution -- focus on *differences*, not just correctness.
3. If your answer is different but equally valid, that is fine. Understand the trade-offs.
4. Read the "Common Mistakes" sections even if you got the exercise right.
